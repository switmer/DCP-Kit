Live-site DCP experiments — dashboard

Two hand-authored experiments against structurally different live sites, with measured auto-mapping run via Get-Site-Styles on 2026-04-20.

Thesis (refined after measurement): Output quality depends on the mapping operation, not a single site property. Human mapping benefits from author-declared semantic signal (variable names, selector provenance). Algorithmic mapping as implemented in GSS benefits from palette richness and saturation separation — regardless of author naming. The strong form of the Webflow-cooperative-sites hypothesis was falsified: GSS scored the stencil-no-semantic-vars site higher than the semantic-var-rich site.
